Muscle Gain Training Program

Back to Plans

This 5-day program is designed for building lean muscle mass, focusing on progressive overload and consistent training. Ensure adequate protein intake and caloric surplus for optimal results.

Day 1: Chest & Triceps

Barbell Bench Press: 3-4 sets of 6-10 reps
Incline Dumbbell Press: 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps
Decline Barbell Press (optional): 3x8-12 reps
Dumbbell Flyes: 3x10-15 reps
Close-Grip Bench Press: 3x8-12 reps
Overhead Dumbbell Extension: 3x10-15 reps
Triceps Pushdowns (Cable): 3x12-15 reps
Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets.

Day 2: Back & Biceps

Pull-ups: 3-4 sets of as many reps as possible
Barbell Rows: 3-4 sets of 6-10 reps
Seated Cable Rows: 3x10-15 reps
Face Pulls: 3x15-20 reps
Barbell Bicep Curls: 3x8-12 reps
Dumbbell Hammer Curls: 3x10-15 reps
Concentration Curls: 3x12-15 reps per arm
Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets.

Day 3: Legs & Shoulders

Barbell Back Squats: 3-4 sets of 6-10 reps
Romanian Deadlifts: 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps
Leg Press: 3x10-15 reps
Walking Lunges (Dumbbell): 3x10-12 reps per leg
Leg Extensions: 3x12-15 reps
Hamstring Curls (Lying): 3x12-15 reps
Standing Calf Raises: 3x15-20 reps
Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets.

Day 4: Active Recovery

Active Recovery: Light activities like walking, cycling at a relaxed pace, swimming, or stretching. This can help with blood flow and reduce muscle soreness.

Day 5: Shoulders & Core

Overhead Press (Dumbbell): 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps
Lateral Raises: 3x12-15 reps
Front Raises (Dumbbell): 3x12-15 reps
Rear Delt Flyes (Dumbbell): 3x15-20 reps
Upright Rows (Dumbbell): 3x10-15 reps
Shrugs (Dumbbell): 3x10-15 reps
Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets.
